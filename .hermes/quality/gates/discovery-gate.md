# Discovery Gate

## When This Gate Is Used

Use after idea discovery and before product planning.

## Required Artifacts

- Idea brief
- Pain point map
- Customer segment
- Opportunity areas
- Validation questions

## Required Checks

- Idea quality rubric
- Pain point quality rubric
- Customer segment quality rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Idea, pain, and segment score 4 or higher.
- Primary user and pain are explicit.
- Opportunity areas are traceable to evidence or assumptions.
- Validation questions are clear enough to answer.

## Block Conditions

- No primary user.
- No clear pain or opportunity.
- Pain is based only on internal opinion.
- Segment is too broad to guide product decisions.

## Recommended Next Action

If passed, continue to `product-planning.yaml`. If blocked, return to idea facilitation or pain-point discovery.
