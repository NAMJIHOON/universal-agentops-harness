# Planning Gate

## When This Gate Is Used

Use after product planning and before UX, marketing, or delivery backlog creation.

## Required Artifacts

- Problem statement
- PRD
- MVP scope
- Success metrics
- Roadmap outline

## Required Checks

- PRD quality rubric
- MVP scope quality rubric
- Customer segment quality rubric

## Pass Criteria

- PRD and MVP scope score 4 or higher.
- Goals, non-goals, and success metrics are explicit.
- MVP scope is small enough to deliver and validate.
- Roadmap separates MVP, near-term, and later work.

## Block Conditions

- PRD is only a feature list.
- MVP contains unbounded or unvalidated scope.
- Success metrics are missing or unmeasurable.
- Key assumptions are hidden.

## Recommended Next Action

If passed, continue to UX and marketing sprint planning. If blocked, refine PRD, metrics, or MVP scope.
